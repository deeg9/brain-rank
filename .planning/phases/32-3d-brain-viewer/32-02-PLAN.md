   - Renders `<mesh>` with `<meshStandardMaterial>`:

     - For base: `color="#0d0d1a"`, `emissive="#1a1a2e"`, `emissiveIntensity={0.05}`, `roughness={0.8}`, `metalness={0.1}`, `transparent`, `opacity={0.4}`
