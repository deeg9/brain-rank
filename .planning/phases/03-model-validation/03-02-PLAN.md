## Header
- Title: "TRIBE v2 Model Validation Report"
- Date, GPU used, videos tested

## Test Setup
- High-engagement video: Sintel trailer (action, emotion, dramatic music, fight scenes)
- Low-engagement video: Big Buck Bunny opening (static nature scene, no dialogue, minimal action)
- Both 60 seconds, processed on A40 GPU
- Region mapping: 4 regions from Phase 2 (attention, reward, emotional_processing, social_cognition)
- Hemodynamic correction: 5-second HRF delay applied

## Per-Region Comparison
A table showing for each region:
| Region | High Mean | Low Mean | Difference | Cohen's d | Effect Size |
Pull all values from data/validation_comparison.json. Format numbers to 4 decimal places.

## Interpretation
For each region, explain what the scores mean in plain language:
- Higher attention activation during action scenes = model detects visual salience
- Higher reward during emotionally compelling content = model detects reward circuit engagement
- etc.

Note: If differences are small or unexpected, document that honestly. The point is honest assessment, not confirmation bias.

## Go/No-Go Decision
Based on the comparison data:
- **GO** if: At least 2 of 4 regions show meaningful differentiation (|Cohen's d| > 0.2), AND the direction makes neuroscientific sense (high-engagement video shows higher activation in attention/emotion regions)
- **NO-GO** if: Fewer than 2 regions differentiate, OR activations go in the wrong direction, OR the model produces essentially identical outputs for both videos
- **CONDITIONAL GO** if: Results are mixed -- some regions differentiate but others do not. Document what works and what does not.

Record the decision clearly with rationale. If NO-GO, note that Phases 4-10 should be reconsidered.

## Limitations
- Only 2 videos tested (small sample)
- Cortical surface only (subcortical structures like amygdala, ventral striatum not captured by TRIBE v2)
- Hemodynamic delay is approximate (5s fixed, actual varies 4-6s)
- Effect size thresholds are conventional, not neuroscience-specific

Pull Phase 3 values from data/validation_comparison.json timing fields.

## GPU Specifications
| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| GPU | NVIDIA A40 |
| VRAM Total | 48 GB |
| VRAM Peak | 11.16 GB (Phase 1 measured) |
| VRAM Headroom | ~37 GB |

## Key Findings for Downstream Phases
1. **Async endpoint required**: ~12-13 min per 60s video rules out /runsync (90s timeout)
2. **Timeout recommendation**: Set execution timeout to 20 minutes (includes headroom for longer videos, cold start)
3. **Cost per inference**: ~$0.07 per 60s video on A40 at $0.35/hr
4. **Model load is fast**: 2.2s -- loading once at container start is effective
5. **Video encoding dominates**: V-JEPA2 video encoding is ~11 min of the ~12 min total
6. **VRAM allows cheaper GPU**: 11 GB peak means A30 (24GB) or RTX 4090 (24GB) could work, pending testing

## Scaling Estimates
- 30s video: ~6-7 min estimated (roughly linear with duration)
- 60s video: ~12-13 min measured
- 120s video: ~24-26 min estimated (may need higher timeout)
- Maximum practical video: ~3-5 min of content before 30-min timeout becomes risky
